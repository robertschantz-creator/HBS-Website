The Guide Behind the Work

When you're in a real transition — career, relationship, a move you can't stop thinking about — you need someone who has actually watched thousands of people navigate these moments. Here's why Robert is that person for you.

Experience That Serves Your Transition

Most of the meaningful transitions in life don't arrive with clear instructions.

They arrive as discomfort, restlessness, or a quiet sense that something is shifting — even when everything on the surface appears fine. If you're in that place right now, you're not broken. You may just be out of phase.

Robert Schantz has spent nearly four decades working in sales, management, and personal development — and nearly two decades as a coach. That work surfaced one pattern over and over: what keeps people stuck isn't a lack of effort. It's a mismatch between what they're pushing toward and what their current phase of life is actually designed for.

That's the work. Not motivating you. Not pushing you harder. Helping you see where you actually are — so the next move makes sense and actually holds.

A Perspective Formed Over Time

One Truth Became Clear

"People don't struggle because they are broken — they struggle because they are out of rhythm with their current phase."

When timing is misunderstood, even good decisions feel heavy. When timing is understood, direction becomes steadier and more sustainable.

Human Behavior Specialists exists to provide that context.

This work is informed by decades of observing human behavior — in business, leadership, relationships, and periods of transition.

Over time, patterns emerged. People in the same kinds of situations — career plateaus, relationship crossroads, identity shifts — were navigating challenges that weren't about effort or intelligence. They were about timing.

The Pythagorean-based numerological framework that forms the foundation of this work provided a structural map for those patterns — a way to see cycles, name phases, and understand what a particular period of life is actually designed for.

That map, combined with direct advisory experience, is what each session draws from.

The Method

Why Numerology? Because I've Always Worked With Numbers.

No crystals. No chimes. No crystal ball.

I spent more than 35 years in sales, finance, and real estate, where every decision ran on numbers. Cap rates. Conversion ratios. Market cycles. Numbers don't flatter you and they don't guess. They show you patterns.

Numerology works the same way.

What is Pythagorean numerology?

It's a system that dates back to the Greek mathematician Pythagoras. Each letter of your birth name and each digit of your birth date converts to a number. Those numbers reduce to a small set of core values that make up your personal blueprint.

What's in your blueprint?

Life Path
The overall direction and lessons of your life
Expression
Your natural talents and how you show up in the world
Soul Urge
What actually motivates you underneath it all
Personality
How others tend to see you first
Personal Year
The cycle you're in right now — and what it's asking of you

Why I use it in coaching

Most people I work with aren't lacking effort. They're pushing hard in the wrong season. They're starting when it's time to finish, or holding on when it's time to let go.

Your Personal Year shows where you are in a nine-year cycle. When you understand your timing, you stop fighting it and start using it.

I don't use numerology to predict your future. I use it the way I used market data for decades: as one more lens to see the pattern clearly so we can build a smarter plan. It sits alongside behavioral tools like DiSC and real conversation about where you want to go.

You're Not Behind. You're Off Timing.

The Philosophy

Every Life Moves Through Distinct Cycles

Some phases are meant for building. Others for consolidating, refining, or letting go. Difficulty often arises when we try to force a phase that has already passed — or rush one that hasn't yet arrived.

BuildingCreation & momentum
ExpandingGrowth & reach
ConsolidatingRefining & deepening
ReleasingCompletion & transition

This work helps people recognize the phase they are in, understand what is naturally completing, identify what is beginning to take shape, and make decisions that align with timing rather than pressure.

The goal is not certainty. The goal is orientation.
