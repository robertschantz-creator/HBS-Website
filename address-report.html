Why a Real Estate Broker Does This

The Numbers Were Always There.

I've spent 25 years in real estate — 12 as Managing Broker, working with 2,000+ agents and thousands of transactions. I've watched clients choose properties based on gut feeling, and sometimes they were right in ways they couldn't explain. The address number is one layer of that.

Pythagorean numerology assigns a value to each digit in an address. Those values reduce to a single core number. That number carries a specific energetic pattern — things it tends to support, and things it tends to create friction around.

I'm not saying to skip the inspection. I'm saying there's a data point worth having before you commit to a space you'll spend years in.

What the Numbers Look For

Address Number 1 — Independence, leadership, new starts. Strong for self-employed people or anyone launching something.

Address Number 4 — Stability, structure, hard work. Great for building. Can feel heavy if you're trying to relax.

Address Number 6 — Family, nurturing, beauty. Classic home energy. Strong for raising children or long-term partnership.
