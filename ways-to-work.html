Most people begin here. It is complete on its own and does not require any ongoing commitment.

02 Depth Work

Life Cycle Mapping

A deeper exploration of the recurring patterns across your life — connecting past cycles to present circumstances and making sense of the larger arc you're living through.

This session looks at your life's structural timeline: where you've been, what patterns have repeated, what cycles have completed, and what the current phase is asking of you given all that came before.

It's especially valuable for people in midlife transitions, major professional shifts, or anyone who senses there's a larger story at play but can't yet name it.

  • Insight into what the current phase is designed for
  • A coherent arc — past, present, emerging future

Best For

  • Midlife or identity transitions
  • Sense of a larger story at play

03 Precision Support

Decision-Timing Consultation

Focused support around a specific decision — not what to choose, but when and how. This session reduces pressure and helps you move from alignment rather than urgency.

Career changes, financial decisions, major investments, relationship crossroads, business pivots — decisions that carry long-term weight benefit enormously from timing awareness.

The goal is not to tell you what to do. It's to help you understand whether now is the moment to act, wait, or reconsider — and why.

Book This Session

What to Expect

  • Focused exploration of a specific decision
  • Timing analysis — act, wait, or reconsider?
  • Pattern context for the decision at hand
  • Reduced pressure around an urgent-feeling choice
  • Clarity on how this decision fits the larger cycle

Common Decision Areas

  • Career transitions and professional pivots
  • Financial and investment timing
  • Real estate and property decisions
  • Relationship and life partner crossroads
  • Business launches or exits

Bring one primary decision. The focus stays on timing intelligence — not on making the decision for you.

04 Integration Work

Next-Chapter Orientation

A short series designed for people entering a meaningful new chapter — after long careers, major transitions, or periods of significant change. Integration, not reinvention.

This is not about becoming someone new. It's about understanding who you are becoming, and aligning the next chapter with what's actually true for you now — not who you were five or ten years ago.

This work is offered selectively, not by default. It is best suited for those who have already engaged in one of the single sessions and are ready to go deeper.

What to Expect

  • 2–3 focused sessions over a defined period
  • Integration of what has completed and what's emerging
  • Values and identity realignment for the next chapter
  • Direction that is sustainable, not just exciting
  • A grounded sense of what's worth building next

This Series Is For

  • Post-career or major transition integration
  • Those entering a legacy or stewardship phase
  • People who want depth, not a quick reset
  • Those already familiar with the HBS approach

Next-Chapter Orientation is a culmination offering, not a starting point. An initial Personal Forecast Session is strongly recommended first.
