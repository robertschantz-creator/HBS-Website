The Four Styles

The DISC Behavioral Model

Four observable patterns of behavior. None better than the others. Each carries a natural pace, a natural focus, and a natural professional environment.

D Dominance
i Influence
C Conscientiousness
S Steadiness
D

Dominance

Results-oriented, direct, decisive. Moves fast under pressure. Thrives on challenge, autonomy, and clear outcomes.

i

Influence

People-oriented, expressive, optimistic. Moves through relationships and energy. Thrives on collaboration, visibility, and momentum.

S

Steadiness

Pace-oriented, calm, deliberate. Moves with consistency and care. Thrives on stability, trust, and meaningful contribution.

C

Conscientiousness

Quality-oriented, analytical, precise. Moves with rigor and accuracy. Thrives on standards, expertise, and well-defined systems.

The HBS Lens

Behavior is the how. Timing is the when. Together they show what fits now.

Behavior — How you operate today

DISC describes how you naturally behave in the present — visible, observable, real. Direct or expressive? Steady or precise? Behavior is not identity. It can shift across roles, environments, and pressures. We read it as a current-state signal, not a fixed label.

Timing — Where you are in the cycle

Your timing read describes the phase of life you are in — what is naturally completing, what is beginning to take shape, what this chapter is designed for. The Personal Year Timing Report is the entry point. It gives the cycle context that behavior alone cannot.

"This is not personality typing. We do not label who you are. We read how you behave — and we pair it with the pattern you are living inside."
