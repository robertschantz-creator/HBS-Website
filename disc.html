The Four Styles

The DISC Behavioral Model

Four observable patterns. None better than the others. Each carries a natural pace, a natural focus, and a natural fit for the environments you'll do your best work in.

D Dominance
i Influence
C Conscientiousness
S Steadiness
Results · Pace · Challenge
D

Dominance

Direct, decisive, results-oriented. Moves fast under pressure and gets impatient when things stall. You thrive on challenge, autonomy, and a clear outcome — and you probably drain in environments built around process and consensus.

People · Energy · Momentum
i

Influence

Expressive, enthusiastic, people-first. You lead with relationships and energy, and the work happens through connection. You thrive on collaboration, visibility, and momentum — and you dim in environments with no one to talk to.

Pace · Trust · Steadiness
S

Steadiness

Calm, patient, loyal. You move at a sustainable pace and you build the kind of relationships and systems that last. You thrive on stability, trust, and meaningful contribution — and you suffer in environments built on constant churn.

Quality · Precision · Standards
C

Conscientiousness

Analytical, careful, standards-driven. You move with rigor and you want to be right, not just fast. You thrive on expertise, well-defined systems, and quality — and you shut down in environments that reward shortcuts over substance.

The HBS Lens
