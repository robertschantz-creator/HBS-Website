What Is a Personal Year — and Why It Changes Everything About Your Decisions

Timing & Cycles Robert Schantz October 2026 7 min read

Most people make decisions based on what they want. The ones who consistently move well make decisions based on where they are in their cycle.

A Personal Year is a numerological cycle that runs from 1 through 9 — and then repeats. Each year in that cycle carries a specific design: things it's built to support, and things it tends to resist. When you know which year you're in, you stop fighting your phase and start working with it.

This isn't about prediction. It's about orientation. The same way a farmer who knows the season doesn't plant in winter, someone who understands their Personal Year doesn't try to launch when their cycle is asking them to consolidate.

"Most people don't have a direction problem. They have a timing problem. They're doing the right things — just in the wrong year."

How to Calculate Your Personal Year

Your Personal Year is calculated by adding your birth month, birth day, and the current calendar year together, then reducing to a single digit.

Example: If you were born on March 15 (3/15) and the current year is 2026:

  • Birth month: 3
  • Birth day: 1 + 5 = 6
  • Current year: 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
  • Total: 3 + 6 + 1 = 10 → Personal Year 1

If your total reaches 11 or 22, those are Master Numbers — they're read differently and carry additional weight. Most totals reduce to a number between 1 and 9.

Your Personal Year shifts on your birthday each year, not on January 1st. So if your birthday is in August, your new Personal Year begins in August — not in January.

What Each Personal Year Is Designed For

Here's a plain-language breakdown of all nine years. Notice that each year builds on the one before it — the cycle has a logic to it when you see them as a sequence.

1

Personal Year 1 — New Beginnings

A year for starting. New projects, new directions, new identities. The cycle is fresh. This is the right year to launch, apply, move toward something you've been circling. Hesitation in a 1 year costs more than action.

2

Personal Year 2 — Partnership & Patience

A slower, more relational year. Decisions that were seeded in the 1 year begin to develop — but they need time. This is a year for collaboration, waiting, and building relationships rather than pushing for solo results.

3

Personal Year 3 — Expression & Expansion

A year for visibility and communication. Creative output, social connection, and putting yourself or your work in front of more people. The 3 year rewards self-expression. It's not the year for quiet, internal work.

4

Personal Year 4 — Structure & Foundation

A year for building the systems underneath the vision. Discipline, organization, practical work. The 4 year feels slower than the 3 — and that's by design. What you build now holds up later. What you skip now falls apart later.

5

Personal Year 5 — Change & Freedom

A year of movement, disruption, and unexpected shifts. Things that were stable may loosen. This is often when people move, change careers, or exit relationships — not always by choice. The 5 year asks for adaptability, not control.

6

Personal Year 6 — Responsibility & Home

A year focused on home, family, and service. Relationships deepen or resolve. Domestic decisions — buying a home, having children, caring for a parent — tend to surface in the 6 year. The 6 asks: where do your commitments actually lie?

7

Personal Year 7 — Rest, Reflection & Inner Work

The most internal year of the cycle. Analysis, study, solitude, and deep questioning. A 7 year is not designed for external output — trying to force expansion in a 7 year tends to produce frustration. This is the year to think, not to launch.

8

Personal Year 8 — Power & Results

A year when effort produces visible results — financially and professionally. What was built in the 4 and developed in the 5 and 6 now has the potential to pay off. The 8 rewards ambition. It also amplifies whatever you've been building, good or bad.

9

Personal Year 9 — Completion & Release

The final year of the cycle. Things that are finished tend to end — relationships, jobs, living situations. This year asks you to let go of what no longer fits so the 1 year can begin fresh. Holding on in a 9 year tends to drag the completion into the next cycle.

Why This Actually Changes How You Make Decisions

When I work with someone who's frustrated — they've been pushing hard and not seeing results — the first thing I look at is their Personal Year. More often than not, the effort is right but the timing is off.

Someone launching a new business in a Personal Year 7 is fighting their cycle. The 7 year is built for internal development, not external launch. That doesn't mean they'll fail — it means they'll work significantly harder than someone doing the same thing in a Personal Year 1 or 8.

Someone trying to hold a relationship together in a Personal Year 9 is often fighting a completion that's already in motion. The question isn't whether to fight harder — it's whether the relationship is meant to complete, or evolve into something new in the 1 year ahead.

"The right move in the wrong year still costs you. Not because the move was wrong — because the timing was."

What a Personal Year Doesn't Tell You

A Personal Year is one layer of a larger picture. It tells you about the energetic design of a given year, but it doesn't tell you everything. Your Life Path, Expression Number, and Personal Month all add nuance — and the interaction between your numbers and a specific decision (a location, a partner, a business venture) adds more.

It also doesn't override agency. Knowing you're in a Personal Year 9 doesn't mean you have to end your relationship or quit your job. It means you should look clearly at what's completing — and make a conscious decision about it, rather than one driven by denial or inertia.

The goal is never to have the numbers make your decisions. The goal is to have better information when you do.
