The 3 Signs This Guide Covers

These aren't dramatic signs. They're quiet ones — the kind most people dismiss, push through, or try to fix with more effort.

01

Something That Used to Fit No Longer Does

Your work, your relationships, your routines — they haven't changed, but the way they feel has. This is one of the clearest signals that a cycle is completing. The guide explains what it means and what to do with it.

02

You're Tired in a Way That Sleep Doesn't Fix

This isn't burnout from overwork. It's a particular exhaustion that comes from operating outside your current cycle. The guide helps you identify whether you're in a consolidation phase — and why pushing harder won't help.

03

You Know Something Is Next — But You Can't Name It

There's a pull. A sense that something is forming. But when you try to articulate it, it slips away. The guide explains why this is not vagueness — it's timing. And why the next step often reveals itself quietly.
